Premier12
WBSC Premier12 · The World's #2 International Baseball Tournament

The second-most prestigious international baseball tournament in the world, behind only the World Baseball Classic. The Premier12 brings together the top 12 baseball nations on Earth for one of the toughest brackets in the sport — and in the LA28 Olympic cycle, this is the qualification pathway. 2026 is qualifier year (Barcelona + Zhongshan); the 16-team main tournament arrives in 2027 with the COVID-delayed expansion finally taking effect. DAZN holds exclusive US streaming rights under an 8-year global WBSC partnership.

Where to Watch the Premier12

2026 Qualifiers + 2027 Main Tournament
Primary · US Exclusive
DAZN
DAZN subscription
DAZN holds exclusive US streaming rights to the Premier12 under the 8-year WBSC partnership signed October 2024. Every game in 2026 qualifiers and the 2027 tournament will require a DAZN subscription for US viewers. Outside the US, Premier12 games are available on DAZN's free tier.
International Distribution
WBSC GameTime
Per-tournament passes
WBSC's official OTT platform (gametime.sport) carries Premier12 in many international markets. Per-tournament passes are typically available for fans outside DAZN-distributed territories.
Local Rights · Asia
Domestic Networks
Country-specific
Japan, Korea, Taiwan, and Mexico have separate local rights holders outside the DAZN/WBSC global deal. Domestic broadcasters in those four markets carry their national teams. NHK in Japan, MBC in Korea, ELTA Sports in Taiwan, ESPN Mexico.
North American Spanish
ESPN Deportes / TUDN
Cable Spanish-language
ESPN Deportes and TUDN often pick up Premier12 games featuring Latin American teams (Mexico, Venezuela, Dominican Republic, Cuba) as part of their Spanish-language sports rights packages.

2026 Qualifier Schedule

Pathway to 2027 main tournament
Barcelona Qualifier · October 9-11, 2026
The European qualifier hosted in Barcelona, Spain. Teams compete for slots in the expanded 16-nation 2027 Premier12. Spain hosts but multiple European nations participate. Watch for Italy, Netherlands, Czechia, and Germany as the perennial European baseball powers, with Spain competing on home soil. Stream: DAZN (US exclusive) and WBSC GameTime.
Zhongshan Qualifier · November 26-28, 2026
The Asia/Pacific qualifier hosted in Zhongshan, China. Multiple Asia and Pacific nations compete for the remaining slots in the 2027 expanded tournament. Watch for China, Hong Kong, Philippines, Pakistan, and other regional baseball developing nations battling for limited spots. Stream: DAZN (US exclusive) and WBSC GameTime.

The Fact
Taiwan Beat Japan at the Tokyo Dome and Nobody in America Noticed
In November 2024, the Chinese Taipei national team did something previously thought impossible: they beat Japan 4-0 at the Tokyo Dome to win the Premier12 championship — Taiwan's first major WBSC title in baseball history, and arguably the most significant moment for Asian baseball outside Japan and Korea this century. The crowd was packed with both Taiwanese supporters who flew in for the final and shocked Japanese fans watching their team get shut out at home. The story was front-page news in Taiwan for weeks. In the United States, where DAZN held the streaming rights, the audience was a fraction of what the WBC pulls — most American baseball fans don't know it happened. The 2027 Premier12, with the expanded 16-nation field, is Taiwan's chance to defend the title they earned in shocking fashion. If you missed it last time, don't miss it next year. DAZN. Mark your calendar.
