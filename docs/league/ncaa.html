Division I College Baseball

The most-covered amateur baseball league in the world, and the second-most-watched amateur sport in the United States after college football. 300+ Division I programs feed the MLB Draft every July. The 2026 championship runs June 12-22, and getting there means surviving conference tournaments, regionals, and super regionals across May and June. WBN covers this beat with 400+ articles to date.

Where to Watch NCAA Baseball

Primary · CWS + Selection
ESPN Networks
Cable / ESPN+ subscription
ESPN owns the College World Series. Every CWS game airs on ESPN, ESPN2, or ESPNU, with the Championship Final on ESPN. ESPN+ carries regionals and super regionals. The NCAA Selection Show on May 25 airs on ESPN.

SEC Network · ACC Network · BTN · ESPNU

The four Power Conference tournaments (May 19-24) air across their respective conference networks. SEC Network for the SEC tournament in Hoover, ACC Network for ACC in Charlotte, BTN for the Big Ten in Omaha, ESPN family for the Big 12 in Surprise, AZ.

Power Conference Tournaments

May 19-24, 2026
SEC Baseball Tournament — Hoover, AL
The deepest conference in college baseball. 14 teams, single-elimination format starting Tuesday. Tennessee, LSU, Arkansas, Texas A&M, Florida, and Vanderbilt are perennial contenders. Winner gets the SEC's automatic NCAA Tournament bid; multiple SEC teams typically reach the College World Series in any given year.
ACC Baseball Tournament — Charlotte, NC
Truist Field is a major-league-quality venue. 12 teams, pool play into single elimination. North Carolina, Virginia, Wake Forest, Florida State, Clemson, and Miami are perennial contenders. ACC has produced multiple recent CWS finalists.
Big 12 Baseball Tournament — Surprise, AZ
Surprise Stadium is the spring training home of the Texas Rangers. 9 teams in 2026. Oklahoma State, TCU, Texas Tech, Kansas State, and West Virginia are perennial contenders. The Big 12 expanded with new members and the tournament reflects that depth.
Big Ten Baseball Tournament — Omaha, NE
Played at the same Charles Schwab Field that hosts the CWS three weeks later — giving the Big Ten champion a built-in venue advantage if they make it back. 8 teams, double elimination. Maryland, Indiana, Michigan, Iowa, Nebraska. The conference is in growth mode after recent expansion.
The Fact
The College World Series Is Where Half of MLB's Roster Was Discovered
Charles Schwab Field in Omaha hosts the College World Series every June and pulls in more than 350,000 cumulative attendees over 10 days — making it one of the most-attended amateur sporting events in the world. The tournament has produced an absurd percentage of the modern MLB workforce: Stephen Strasburg, Bryce Harper, Mike Trout (sort of — he didn't play in the CWS but became its highest-profile draft alum), Kris Bryant, Trevor Bauer, Carlos Rodón, Adley Rutschman, Paul Skenes, and on and on. Every June, 8 teams converge on Omaha and the eventual winner becomes part of a 75-year tradition that connects to MLB more directly than any other amateur tournament on Earth. The MLB Draft three weeks later is, in part, a postscript to what happens here.
