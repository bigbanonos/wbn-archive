Italia The Cinderella Roster

30 Players · 5-1 Tournament Record · Lost SF to Venezuela 4-2

The story of WBC 2026. Italy went undefeated through pool play in Houston, knocked out Australia in the quarterfinal, then lost a hard-fought 4-2 decision to eventual champions Venezuela in the Miami semifinal. The roster is a brilliant blend of Italian-born FIBS Serie A pipeline talent (Sam Aldegheri, Claudio Scotti, Gabriele Quattrini), Italian-American MLB stars (Aaron Nola, Adam Ottavino, Michael Lorenzen, Vinnie Pasquantino, Jon Berti), and a young breakout core (Jac Caglianone, Andrew Fischer, Dante Nori, Kyle Teel) that signals where Team Italy is headed for 2030. Manager Mike Piazza has built something durable. This is the future of European baseball, fielded today.
