Dominican Republic The Stacked Roster

30 Players · 5-1 Tournament Record · 1.025 OPS · 1.98 ERA

The deepest roster in the tournament. Vladimir Guerrero Jr.'s 1.421 OPS. Junior Caminero's 3 home runs at 22 years old. Fernando Tatis Jr. with 11 RBI. Manny Machado the elder statesman with a .481 OBP. Juan Soto, Ketel Marte, Julio Rodríguez. The pitching staff posted a 1.98 ERA — the best mark of any team that played a full bracket — anchored by Brayan Bello, Luis Severino, Cristopher Sánchez, and an MLB closer-stacked bullpen of Doval, Domínguez, Estévez, Peralta, and Soto. They won pool play. They demolished South Korea 10-0 in the QF. They lost a 2-1 heartbreaker to USA in the SF — Henderson and Roman Anthony solo HRs for USA, Caminero's HR for DR — that ended the deepest roster's championship run.
